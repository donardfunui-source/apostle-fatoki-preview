Who We Are

Carrying the Gospel to the Nations

Apostle Fatoki Global Outreach Inc. began as a simple conviction: that the local church and the global mission field are not two separate things, but one calling. What started as a small outreach has grown into a ministry that spans crusades, church services, media broadcasts, compassion work, and a school raising up the next generation of leaders.

Today, the ministry serves a home congregation while reaching believers and seekers in dozens of nations through television, streaming, and citywide crusades. Every arm of the ministry exists for one purpose: to see lives genuinely transformed by the power of Jesus Christ.

We believe the Gospel is for everyone, that discipleship is a lifelong journey, and that every believer has a part to play in the Great Commission. Whether you are taking your first step of faith or have walked with God for decades, there is a place for you in this family.

Founder & Lead Pastor

Apostle Samuel Fatoki

Apostle Samuel Fatoki leads Apostle Fatoki Global Outreach Inc. with a mandate to see individuals, families, and nations transformed through the Gospel. His ministry combines bold, apostolic preaching with a shepherd's heart for practical discipleship.

Over nearly two decades of ministry, he has preached across crusades, conferences, and church platforms on multiple continents, with a significant focus on raising leaders through the Apostle Fatoki School of Ministry, equipping the next generation to carry the work forward.
