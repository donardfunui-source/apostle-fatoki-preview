Our Story

Transforming Lives Through Christ

Apostle Fatoki Global Outreach is a global ministry committed to transforming lives through the power of Jesus Christ. We preach the Gospel, equip believers and impact nations through crusades, media, and training.

Through the Apostle Fatoki School of Ministry, we train men and women to carry the message of Christ into their churches, communities and nations.

Apostle Samuel Fatoki

Founder & President

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, equip believers and impact nations through crusades, media, and training.

Our Vision

Transformed lives and equipped leaders carrying the message of Christ to every nation.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

01

The Word of God

Every message we preach and every class we teach is anchored in Scripture.

02

We take the Gospel beyond church walls through crusades, outreach and media.

03

We raise believers into mature, equipped leaders.

04

Prayer

Prayer is the foundation of every work of this ministry.

05

Integrity

We handle God's work and every gift entrusted to us with honesty.

06

Global Impact

We serve cities and nations with the love and power of Christ.
